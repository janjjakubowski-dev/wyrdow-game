# Town 2 Design Brief — Miedźno, the Copper Hill

*Synthesized from the five-lens design panel (sprint 1). Base concept: CONTINUITY (Miedźno/Skarbnik).
Major graft: VISUAL (the Sleeper). Forest concepts (Borów / Matecznik) reserved for Act 3.
Decisive fact: Act 1's shipped dialogue already names Miedźno, the copper hill, Srulik, and
"the golem" (index.html lines 262, 709, 834) — Town 2 was canon before the panel sat.*

---

## Pitch

Miedźno is the copper hill Baba pointed you to — except the hill is not a hill. It is a
**golem the size of a town**, lying down for eleven years with the truth scraped off its
forehead, and Miedźno lives in the crook of its arm. The town keeps memory in metal,
because paper burns and ink can be erased. The Regency has escalated from one polite
officer to a **Requisition Office** with a smoking crucible, melting the town's folk
copper into quota — and every charm that goes into the pot takes a name out of the world.

## Patron spirit(s) — two traditions, one hill

- **The Sleeper** *(Chasidic clay-guardian canon)* — the recumbent golem whose head and
  shoulder form the NE terraces. Its heartbeat is a slow knock in the rock, once every
  breath-length; the town knocks back each evening. The **emet→met** erasure (the scraped
  forehead) is the town's central wound and the fragment quest's engine.
- **The Skarbnik** *(Silesian mining folklore)* — the Treasurer, spirit of the sealed mine
  beneath. Bread left on the shrine ledge, no whistling below, lamp etiquette. He keeps
  what the town cannot afford to have found.

The bible's thesis in one landmark: the Slavic spirit below, the Chasidic guardian above,
one hill, both banned.

## Map (24×24)

Diagonal-terrace composition (vs Wyrdów's flat radial crossroads). Entered from the **west**
("Wyrdów ←" signpost mirroring Act 1's east signpost). The Sleeper's head/shoulder occupy
the NE high corner in three walkable terraces — a clay face in profile gazing SW across
the town, verdigris tear-streaks below the eye. Town in the arm's crook: the lamp house,
the shared bread kiln, The Ladder (miners' tavern/boarding house), the study house, the
sealed mine gate with the Skarbnik shrine. SW low corner: the Regency Requisition Office —
zinc, straight lines, the crucible's smoke the only un-beautiful smoke on the map.

## Fragment quest — "What Copper Keeps"

1. **Arrival** — travel interstitial east from Wyrdów: road vignette, thinning forest,
   and off-path a stitched waymark + distant campfire (Baba's camp — *seen, not entered*;
   the forest hook consciously deferred to Act 3). Entry checkpoint: the player is logged,
   politely, by Vosk and Pin.
2. **Srulik** — Baba's referral pays off; he mirrors Ezra's codex beat, then deflects.
   The fragment is "kept where copper keeps."
3. **The knock mechanic** *(new core mechanic — not a knot repeat)* — learn knock-patterns
   (from Mendel, the kiln, the lamp house); during the evening Hour of Knocking, answer
   the Sleeper's heartbeat at listening points on the terraces. Wrong rhythm: nothing.
   Right rhythm: the hill remembers a little more.
4. **The three strokes** — the scraped **aleph** must be rewritten: *memory* (Golda's
   lamps), *grief* (Srulik's confession), *truth* (Hana's mirror-ledger) — three quest
   chains, any order, each with a Regency complication.
5. **The forehead** — climb the terraces at night, rewrite the aleph knock by knock.
   The Sleeper's eye opens — one tear of verdigris falls. Fragment 2 is inside it,
   kept eleven years.
6. **Departure hook** — Vosk's requisition ledger is short by exactly one entry.
   Pin looks at the player. Says nothing. Writes nothing.

## Rituals (hidden, folk-logic discoverable)

- **The Skarbnik's Table** — tear a crust, leave it on the mine-shrine ledge; never whole loaves
- **The Answering Knock** — join the town's evening knock during the Hour of Knocking (21:00–22:00)
- **First Loaf for the Sleeper** — bake in Hana's communal kiln; the first loaf goes up the terraces
- **The Quiet Shelf** *(graft, CONTINUITY)* — at night in the lamp house, stand silent while Golda hums

## Visual identity

**Moonwash + verdigris** — the inversion of Wyrdów's dusk-amber. Oxidized teal-green,
rust-orange stained rock, wet slate, soot black; **lamp-gold** the sole warm accent, so
every lit window and lamp reads as defiance. The town sits in the Sleeper's shadow —
no clean daylight, per the bible.

## Audio direction

Hammered dulcimer over the Act 1 hurdy-gurdy bed; the Sleeper's heartbeat as a sub-bass
knock woven into ambient; new SFX set: knock patterns (player + town + Sleeper), crucible
hiss, lamp-house hum, mine-gate groan.

## LBA-mandate amendment *(blessed direction, 2026-07-12)*

The implied Regency furniture is now explicit quest machinery:

- **Entry checkpoint** at the west gate demands a **travel permit** — the player's first
  permit is issued in the shipped Wyrdów return (Brel's office stamp, or Pin's first
  "miscount" if attention ran high). Papers, presented, every entry.
- **Enumerator patrols** walk visible routes through the town (2–3 routes, day-scheduled).
  Caught somewhere restricted (the terraces after curfew, the crucible yard) → escorted
  to the **Records Office** interior, signed out in triplicate; Regency Attention +1,
  small confiscation. Never a game-over.
- **Quiet Step** stance is how the town lives: villagers drop into it near patrols —
  taught by watching, like the noon silence.
- **Interiors** (small TownDefinitions): The Ladder's common room, the Records Office,
  the lamp house. Srulik's study house if budget allows.
- **The terraces use z-levels** — first elevation in the game; knock-listening points
  sit on different heights.
- **Charm economy debut**: surrendered-charm queue at the crucible is the tragedy;
  hidden poppy-seed charms in nooks are the resistance. Charms barter for favors.

## Open questions for the creator

1. **The Sleeper's origin** — built to protect the town from the Collapse, or something
   the Collapse woke? (Affects Act 5's cure lore.)
2. **Vosk's tally** — is she a proto-defector like Pin, or the study in certainty Brel
   only hinted at? One of them should be irredeemable.
3. **Fragment 2's content** — the codex piece itself: recipe fragment, or a name-list?
4. **Scope check** — the knock mechanic needs a small rhythm-input system (~E-taps against
   a visible/audible beat). Approve before build; a fallback is timed-hold like observation.
5. Boy's name: **Mendel** or **Tobiasz** (both panels proposed both, for different NPCs).
